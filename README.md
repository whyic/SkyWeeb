Enhance your discord activity looks with tons of anime icons or hypixel logo and much more!
To configure it, run ``/rpc``.

### Features

- Custom Displays: Freely choose elements to highlight your active profile stats or other customized data.
- Multi-Element Rotation: Cycle through multiple choices dynamically when you assign more than one element per line.

### Mod Compatibility

#### SkyBlocker

- Overlap: SkyBlocker comes bundled with its own Discord RPC system.
- Note: This is harmless, though you may notice overlapping updates unless you turn one off.
